# Owner

See the owner and partners table in `pilot-90-day.md`. Summary:
- **Lead:** Transportation Services, which owns the traffic count program and the expressway detector data.
- **Partners:** City Planning, TTC, Technology Services; the privacy office in an advisory role.
- **For any actual charge:** the Province must legislate (City of Toronto Act s.41 as re-enacted in 2025). Council can request that legislation; it cannot authorise a charge itself.
