# Intercept protocol (3–5 minutes)

**Consent line (read aloud):** "We're a buildathon team studying whether congestion pricing could work in Toronto. Can I ask you five quick questions? We won't record your name or contact details. Is it OK to quote what you say without your name?"

Record one row per answer in `log.csv`. Record self-described respondent type only (e.g. "drives in from Mississauga", "TTC rider", "city staff"). Never record names, emails or phone numbers unless the person explicitly asks to be credited (`consent_to_name = yes`).

1. How do you usually get downtown, and roughly when?
2. If driving into downtown cost about $12 in the morning peak, what would you do? (keep driving and pay / change route / change time / switch to transit, bike or walk / not go)
3. Who do you think would be hurt most by a charge like this?
4. What would make it feel fair? (exemptions, discounts, what the money pays for)
5. What would you want to see before you'd support or oppose it?
6. *(Optional)* Anything we're missing?
